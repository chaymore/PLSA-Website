Explore Law

The Law Experience

Navigating the process of getting into law school and then continuing your career within the law can be confusing, but we are here to help you! This page explains the law process in detail to better help you make informed decisions and create a law career for yourself!

The Journey

The Path Into a Law Career

From joining PLSA to practicing law, here's what the journey typically looks like.

1

Join PLSA

Get involved early. Attend events, connect with mentors, and start building the network and knowledge you'll need for law school applications.

2

Prepare for the LSAT

Study for and take the LSAT, one of the most important factors in law school admissions. Check out our LSAT Resources page for prep materials.

3

Apply to Law School

Build your application: personal statement, letters of recommendation, resume, and transcripts. Research schools that fit your goals.

4

Attend Law School (J.D.)

Complete your Juris Doctor degree, typically 3 years, including coursework, internships, and often a summer associate position.

5

Pass the Bar Exam

Take and pass the bar exam in the state where you plan to practice, allowing you to be licensed as an attorney.

6

Begin Practicing Law

Start your legal career at a firm, government agency, nonprofit, or in-house counsel role, in your chosen area of law.

Practice Areas

Types of Law

The legal field is broad. Here's a quick look at some common practice areas.

Criminal Law

Deals with offenses against the state or public, including prosecution and defense of individuals accused of crimes.

Corporate Law

Focuses on business formation, mergers, contracts, and compliance for companies and organizations.

Family Law

Covers legal matters like divorce, custody, adoption, and other issues within family relationships.

Constitutional Law

Interprets and applies the U.S. Constitution, addressing rights, government powers, and civil liberties.

Intellectual Property Law

Protects creations of the mind, patents, trademarks, and copyrights, from unauthorized use.

Environmental Law

Regulates how individuals and businesses interact with the environment, including pollution and conservation.

Immigration Law

Governs the process of entering, living in, and gaining citizenship within a country.

Tax Law

Covers rules around taxation for individuals and businesses at the federal, state, and local level.
