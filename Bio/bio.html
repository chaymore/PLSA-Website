About PLSA

A welcoming place to explore law, prepare for admission, and find people who can help along the way.

Why we’re here

Turning curiosity about law into informed action.

BYU’s Pre-Law Student Association is a PPAC club for students who are considering law school or a legal career. No particular major, class year, or level of certainty is required.

Through expert panels, LSAT support, application guidance, community events, and professional connections, PLSA makes the path to law more practical, understandable, and collaborative.
